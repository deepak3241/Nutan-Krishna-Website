Our Story

Twelve years of hospitality, one quiet promise

The Royal Stay opened in 2014 in a restored haveli near Udaipur's old town, built around a simple idea — a hotel should feel like a well-kept home, not a transaction. Every room is dressed by hand, every breakfast table is set fresh, and our longest-serving staff member has been welcoming guests at our door for nine years running.
